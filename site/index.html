Suggested bets: the model blends his last 10 games (last 5 count double), his per-minute rate times tonight's projected minutes, and FanDuel Research's projection, then compares that with each book's price after removing the vig. Strong = 10%+ expected value per dollar; Lean = 4%+. Odds also need to be in the model's range to count, and picks stop once a game tips off. These are statistical leans, not sure things.
